# Screenshots and graphics — Camiguin Market

What Play Console needs, and the exact shots to take. None of these exist yet:
take them on a real phone with real listings, not on an emulator with fake data.

## Required formats

| Asset | Size | Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit | 1 | No rounded corners; Play applies the mask |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500 PNG/JPEG | 1 | Shown at the top of the listing, no text in the safe area |
| Phone screenshots | 1080 × 1920 or larger, 16:9–9:16 | 2–8 | JPEG or 24-bit PNG |
| Tablet screenshots | 1600 × 2560 or larger | optional | Only if tablet support is claimed |
| Promo video | YouTube URL | optional | — |

Screenshots must show the app as it is. Do not mock up features that are not
shipped, and do not show other people's phone numbers or messages.

## Shots to take (in this order)

1. **Home / market list** — a full grid of real Camiguin listings, with the
   search bar and categories visible. This is the first impression.
2. **Listing detail** — photos, price, condition, seller name, and the pickup
   map pin in one frame.
3. **Post a listing** — the sell form with 2–3 photos attached and the price
   filled in, to show how simple posting is.
4. **Map view** — pins across the island, so the "local" promise is obvious.
5. **Chat** — a short buyer–seller conversation about a pickup.
6. **Orders** — the seller's order list with a confirmed order.
7. **Profile / account deletion** — the profile screen, ideally showing the
   🗑️ control, which supports the data-deletion declaration.

## Suggested captions

Play does not render captions, but keep them if you build a promo graphic:
"Sell what you grow", "Find it nearby", "Chat before you meet".

## Checklist before uploading

- [ ] All photos and names in the screenshots are the tester's own or consented.
- [ ] No placeholder text visible (`YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE`, `Lorem ipsum`).
- [ ] Version shown anywhere in the UI matches 0.8.
- [ ] Icon and feature graphic reviewed at small size (the icon is often shown
      at 48 dp).
