## Full description

```
Camiguin Market is the local marketplace for the island of Camiguin. Sell what
you grow, catch, cook, or no longer need, and find what your neighbours are
selling — without leaving the island's group chats.

BUY
• Browse by category, town, and price, or search by name.
• See the seller's photos, price, condition, and pickup point on the map.
• Save favourites, add items to a cart, and place an order in a few taps.
• Chat with the seller in the app before you meet.

SELL
• Post a listing with up to 5 photos, a price, and a description.
• Show buyers where to pick up, with a pin on the map.
• Mark perishable items with an expiry so old listings leave the market by
  themselves.
• Track views, cart adds, and orders for each listing.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
• Towns of Camiguin, from Mambajao to Sagay.
• Prices in pesos, listings in Cebuano and English.
• Works on mobile data; photos are resized before they upload.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
• Location is optional and only used when you pin a pickup point or ask for
  directions.
• You can delete your account and its listings inside the app, or by email.
• No ads, and no selling of your data.

Camiguin Market is built for the island's small sellers: fisherfolk, farmers,
home cooks, and sari-sari stores. If something does not work, tell us — the
support address is below.
```
