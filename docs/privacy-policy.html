Privacy Policy

Camiguin Market · com.camiguinmarket.app · Last updated 2026-11-16

This policy explains what Camiguin Market collects, why, and how you can control it. Camiguin Market is a local marketplace app for the island of Camiguin. It is operated by an independent developer and is not affiliated with any government or tourism office.

1. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work.

We do not collect payment or financial information (orders are arranged directly between buyer and seller), advertising identifiers, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, health data, or browsing history.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To show and deliver your listings to other users.
  • To let buyers and sellers chat and arrange a pickup.
  • To send order and chat notifications you opted into.
  • To keep the marketplace safe (reporting, moderation).

3. Processors (third parties)

We use a small number of services to run the app. They only process your data on our behalf and do not sell it.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) — accounts, listings, messages, notification tokens.
  • Cloudinary — storage and delivery of listing photos and profile pictures.
  • OpenStreetMap / OSRM / Nominatim — map tiles, route calculation, and place lookups.

4. Location

Location permission is optional. Browsing, searching, and posting a listing without a pickup pin all work without it. When granted, your location is used only to centre the map, show distance, or draw a route. It is never tracked in the background.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not run ads. Information you put in a listing (title, price, photos, pickup town, seller name) is visible to other users of the app, because that is the point of a marketplace. Your email is shown on a listing only as a contact option if you choose to include it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Listings stay until you delete them, or until a perishable listing expires (the app then removes the listing and its photos automatically).
  • You can delete your account and all of its data inside the app: press the 🗑️ button in the top bar of the market screen.
  • If you no longer have access to the app, email bernavicg1@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. We reply within 7 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Firebase keeps backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they expire.

7. Security

All traffic is over HTTPS. The Cloudinary signing key exists only on our server and is never shipped in the app. Access to your data is restricted to you and to other users only for the parts you choose to publish.

8. Children

Camiguin Market is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. For material changes we will also notify users in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: email bernavicg1@gmail.com.
